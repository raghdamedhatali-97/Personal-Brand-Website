# Storyboard: "Growing" (the website's hero loop)

Logline: Clawd wants a row of seedling bar-charts to grow, so it waters them, and they shoot up into a rising chart.
World: a sunny paper-toned meadow; sky blue fading to cream, sap-green ground, the bars in teal, ochre, rose and clay.
Washes only (no watercolour fills), so it renders quickly in `--lite` mode on machines without a GPU.
Motif: growth. Four seedlings become four bars, then a trend arrow ties them together.
Clawd's arc: thinking → idea → hopeful → starstruck → proud → happy.
Length: 8 s at 120 bpm (16 beats). It loops: the brush wipe that closes it is completed by the wipe that opens it.

Shots:
  A  0.0–8.0  [in: brush wipe, second half]  Clawd beside four seedlings, a watering can hanging from one arm.
     reads:  0.3–1.2  Clawd looks at the tiny seedlings, thinking
             1.2–2.0  a bulb: the idea; Clawd lifts the can (anticipation)
             2.0–4.3  Clawd pours; each bar shoots up on a beat (2.3, 2.8, 3.3), drops arc from the spout
             3.8–4.55 the last bar grows slowest and tallest, landing with a flare and sparkles (the payoff)
             4.6–5.3  a trend arrow paints itself across the tops; Clawd is starstruck (reaction after the cause)
             5.7–6.3  proud: a hop with the arm thrown up
             6.7–7.7  happy, waving at the viewer
  [out: brush wipe, first half; covers the frame at 8.0 = 0.0]
