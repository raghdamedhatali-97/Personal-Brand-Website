01 About

I work in financial planning & analysis: the part of finance that looks forward. I build the budget, keep the forecast honest, and explain what moved and why, so leaders can act on it.

What I care about most is clarity. A model nobody trusts is worthless, and a slide nobody understands is noise. I like models that reconcile, numbers that tie, and a story that a busy executive can read in thirty seconds.

Outside the spreadsheet, I write about FP&A craft: practical cheat-codes for analysts who want to think like owners.
